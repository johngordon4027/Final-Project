Why I Chose to Open a Store

I chose to open a store because I want to create something that reflects my values and work ethic rather than simply contributing to someone else’s vision. In Project Management for the Unofficial Project Manager, the authors explain that successful projects require clear structure, defined goals, and accountability. One concept that stands out to me is the “cadence of accountability.” This idea emphasizes regular check-ins and measurable progress rather than waiting until the end to evaluate results. If I were to start my own business, I would apply this concept by setting weekly performance goals, reviewing financial metrics, and adjusting strategy as needed. I understand that entrepreneurship requires discipline, not just motivation.
